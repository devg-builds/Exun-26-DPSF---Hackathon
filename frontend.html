The pages below are the evidence in the order the agents met it. Read them the way they did.

Exhibit 1 · The government debrief

From: Paul Boiler, Government of nuxEland. To: Conis Nikrobo, Extradigital Exceptions and Unknowns Network.

Wenoti Arcade on 92nd Avenue is drawing many times its normal current from the district supply. The owner made no changes. A transcript suggests a link to the machine “The Reckoning V: Origins”. Run forensics on the physical and digital evidence once sanctions arrive.

Viv S.: Kept from the public to avoid chaos. A federal case was opened only to quiet the Senate.
Exhibit 2 · Energy dossier

Department of Electricity, daily usage for Wenoti Arcade. Compressor startup, elevated harmonics and a small phase imbalance were noted, none established.

Tap a bar.
Viv S.: normal is about 25 kWh. On the 23rd it was 2,684. That is Michael becoming human. Nothing else was noticed.
Exhibit 3 · Transcript A · 23 Oct, 22:31

Nano: Hey, pal. Cool if I play after you?

POI-1 (thick accent): Uh, no. It is not working properly.

Nano: What’s wrong with it?

POI-1 (stuttering): Uh, don’t know.

Nano: What are you trying to do then?

POI-1 (looks at bare wrist): Oh! Look at the time! Really must go.

The machine “warbled” afterwards.

Dev G.: elf ears and armour. Not a cosplayer. Michael, in costume, a blue shirt underneath. He had never learned to be a person.
Exhibit 4 · Asset integrity report

Scuffs on the casing, a slightly offset lower panel, worn fasteners, a dark patch near one vent. Exterior only. Photos redacted. “None of this seems conclusive.”

Dev G.: of course. He did not break out of the cabinet, he glitched through it. The damage was never going to be on the outside.

“A VIDEO GAME CHARACTER HAS ESCAPED INTO THE REAL WORLD!!!! CALL THE MILITARY!!”

Viv S.: nobody believed him. We did. His terminal is still logged in. Nano and Mega have not been found.

Exhibit 8 · The island and the after-action report

Located: a hint in the evidence pointed to an island. Michael was there.

Engaged: gunfire and a hard fight. Michael captured.

Fix: the Character class was rewritten. Michael was sent back into the game and programmed as a plain NPC under the clamp.

Follow-up: every arcade was physically modified so nothing can leave again.

Dev G.: case closed. So why does this file feel unfinished?
Findings
  1. Michael is BLUESHIRT. The machine ID decodes to “blueshirt”. An NPC never changes his clothes.confirmed
  2. The 23 Oct spike was his transformation. 2,684 kWh against a normal 25.inferred
  3. The deleted class is the door. The code clamps every character inside a 24 by 12 grid. Delete the class and nothing holds him.confirmed in code
  4. Someone altered the 24 Oct record. The table says 503.2 kWh, the meter readings say 30.7. Michael’s money reaches the Department.inferred
  5. POI-1 was Michael. The accent, the stutter and the glance at a bare wrist fit a character with no practice at being human.inferred
  6. An island. A hint in the evidence led the agents to him.confirmed
  7. Agents Nano and Mega are still unaccounted for. Nano’s terminal session was never closed.open
